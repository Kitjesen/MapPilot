# Landscape texture sources

The campus's baked landscape materials derive from the four 2048-pixel JPEG
textures below. Their originals remain in the Blender authoring workspace;
this runtime package contains the generated lightmaps in
`2.0.0/visual/native/`, not the source JPEGs or a network dependency.
No third-party models or preview renders are included.

| Local files | Source and authors | Source scale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `bark_willow_diff_2k.jpg`, `bark_willow_disp_2k.jpg` | [Bark Willow](https://polyhaven.com/a/bark_willow), Dario Barresi / Dimitrios Savva | 1 m tile |
| `leafy_grass_diff_2k.jpg`, `leafy_grass_disp_2k.jpg` | [Leafy Grass](https://polyhaven.com/a/leafy_grass), Charlotte Baglioni | 2 m tile |

[CC0 deed](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
The publisher permits commercial use and redistribution. Retrieved 2026-09-08.

Download paths use the official asset host:

```text
https://dl.polyhaven.org/file/ph-assets/Textures/jpg/2k/bark_willow/bark_willow_diff_2k.jpg
https://dl.polyhaven.org/file/ph-assets/Textures/jpg/2k/bark_willow/bark_willow_disp_2k.jpg
https://dl.polyhaven.org/file/ph-assets/Textures/jpg/2k/leafy_grass/leafy_grass_diff_2k.jpg
https://dl.polyhaven.org/file/ph-assets/Textures/jpg/2k/leafy_grass/leafy_grass_disp_2k.jpg
```

Diffuse images are interpreted as sRGB; height images are non-colour data.
The original physical tile sizes are retained. Procedural short-grass geometry
and the continuous trunk/root flare remain LingTu-authored.
